\chapter{Data Model Summary}
\label{annexe:schema}
% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The data model is defined in \texttt{prisma/schema.prisma} using the Prisma schema language. It consists of eight application-defined entities whose primary fields and relations are summarised in Table~\ref{tab:schema-entities}. Authentication-infrastructure models managed by NextAuth.js (\texttt{Account}, \texttt{Session}, \texttt{VerificationToken}) and the system-level \texttt{RateLimit} table are excluded from this summary. The full schema file is available in the project repository.

\begin{table}[H]
  \caption{Prisma schema entities and primary fields}
  \label{tab:schema-entities}
  \centering\small
  \begin{tabular}{|p{2.4cm}|p{7.5cm}|p{3.5cm}|}
    \hline
    \textbf{Entity} & \textbf{Key fields} & \textbf{Relations} \\
    \hline
    Operator     & id, name, slug, logoUrl, websiteUrl, primaryColor & Offer, ScrapeLog \\
    \hline
    Offer        & id, operatorId, name, slug, type, priceDA, dataGB, voiceMinutes, smsCount, validityDays, network, features, isActive, sourceUrl, scrapedAt & Operator, PriceHistory, SavedOffer \\
    \hline
    PriceHistory & id, offerId, priceDA, recordedAt & Offer \\
    \hline
    User         & id, name, email, emailVerified, passwordHash, role, createdAt & UserProfile, SavedOffer, Notification \\
    \hline
    UserProfile  & id, userId, monthlyBudget, dataUsageGB, voiceMinutes, smsCount, preferredType, preferredNet, preferredOperator, priorities & User \\
    \hline
    SavedOffer   & id, userId, offerId, savedAt & User, Offer \\
    \hline
    Notification & id, userId, title, message, type, isRead, offerId, createdAt & User \\
    \hline
    ScrapeLog    & id, operatorId, status, offersFound, offersAdded, offersUpdated, offersDeactivated, details, errorMessage, duration, startedAt, completedAt & Operator \\
    \hline
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}
